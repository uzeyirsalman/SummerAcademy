Welcome to Dad's Summer Academy

A structured, interactive summer camp covering computer literacy, financial economics, physical training, and basic automotive engineering. Click on any active course below to launch the presentation slides.

10 Lessons

Computer Literacy

Trace data flows under the hood of physical hardware, map the plumbing of the global internet, build automated financial spreadsheets, and secure your accounts against engineering attacks.
6 Lessons

Economics 101

Deconstruct how financial markets manage scarcity, calculate the math of compounding growth and interest, design personal budgets, audit credit models, and explore the global stock market.
4 Lessons

Soccer Strategy & Tactics

Analyze the historical evolution of tactical formations (tiki-taka, gegenpressing), diagram plays on a digital whiteboard, structure physical conditioning drills, and explore modern team metrics.

5 Lessons

Cars & Basic Auto Mechanics

Study the 4-stroke internal combustion cycle vs. electric powertrains, understand safety mechanics (brakes, steering), practice emergency tire/battery replacement, and calculate car buying budgets.
