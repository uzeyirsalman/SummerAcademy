ECON101: Economics 101

A structured 6-week curriculum for academic-achieving teenagers. Deconstruct how financial markets manage scarcity, calculate the magic math of compounding growth, build active personal cash flow budgets, audit debt instruments, and trace the stock market.

Coming Soon 6 Weeks Parent-Led
Week 1

Scarcity & Markets

Deconstruct how societies manage limited resources to fulfill unlimited wants. Explore supply and demand interactions, equilibrium price signals, and opportunity cost tradeoffs.

Hands-On Lab The Market Simulation Game (trading commodities to find the market price).
Week 2

The Magic of Compounding

Master the mathematical superpower of compounding returns. Explore the impacts of inflation, distinguish real vs. nominal growth, and calculate exponential interest curves.

Hands-On Lab Spec out growth models mapping $100 deposits over 10, 20, and 40 years.
Week 3

Budgeting & Cash Flow

Take command of personal cash flows. Classify fixed vs. variable expenses, evaluate revenue generation strategies, and design automated personal saving metrics.

Hands-On Lab Design and format a functional spreadsheet tracker managing teen expenses.
Week 4

Deconstructing Credit

Demystify credit scores, interest rate amortization, credit card agreements, borrow economics, and the mathematical mechanics of compounding debt traps.

Hands-On Lab Audit three mock credit agreements to calculate the true cost of repayment.
Week 5

The Global Stock Market

Explore public corporate equity. Deconstruct stock pricing models, index funding configurations (like the S&P 500), and the architecture of stock market exchanges.

Hands-On Lab Build an equity allocation portfolio and trace stock valuations over time.
Week 6

Trade & Global Economy

Analyze economic cooperation. Deconstruct comparative advantage, the mechanics of imports and exports, tariffs, and the fluctuations of global currency exchange rates.

Hands-On Lab The Import-Export Negotiation Challenge (trading resource packages with tariff rules).
